Patient Intake Guide

A practical, field-by-field guide to recording patient details, symptoms and relevant history clearly and consistently in ENT-Care.

Audience: authorised hospital and specialist staff Version: 1.0 Issued: November 2026

Purpose of this guide

Use this guide while completing the three-step digital intake form or the printed blank form. It explains what each field is for, what information to record, and how to complete the intake safely.

Intake is documentation, not diagnosis.

Record information the patient reports and relevant history. Do not use the intake form to make or imply a diagnosis. Clinical assessment and decisions remain the responsibility of a qualified clinician.

Before you begin

  • Sign in with your own authorised hospital account and confirm the correct facility.
  • Confirm the patient's identity using your facility's approved procedure.
  • Explain what information is being recorded and obtain the consent required by your facility and the ENT-Care Terms of Use.
  • Collect only information needed for the patient's care, and enter it where others cannot view it unnecessarily.
  • If urgent warning symptoms are present, follow emergency procedures immediately; do not delay care to finish the form.

The three steps

  1. Patient details
  2. Symptoms and concerns
  3. Health history and attachments

How long it takes

The workspace estimates 3–5 minutes. Allow more time when the patient has multiple symptoms or relevant history.

ENT-Care Hospital Workspace Step 1 of 3
01

Patient details

Enter identifying and contact details accurately. Required fields are marked with an asterisk.

FieldWhat to enter
First name RequiredEnter the patient's given name as recorded by your facility. Avoid nicknames unless that is the only name the patient can provide; follow local identity procedures.
Last name RequiredEnter the patient's family name. Check spelling with the patient or their authorised representative.
Date of birth RequiredSelect the full date of birth using the date control. Confirm the day, month and year; do not estimate. If the date is unknown, follow your facility's intake procedure rather than entering a guessed date.
Sex RequiredSelect the option that the patient provides or that is recorded under your facility's procedure: Female, Male, Intersex, or Prefer not to say. Do not infer this from appearance.
Phone number RequiredEnter a reachable contact number, including the country code when appropriate (for example, +234). Check the digits with the patient. The form requires at least seven characters.
Email address OptionalEnter an address only if the patient provides one and it is relevant. Check its spelling; if provided, it must be in a valid email format.
Residential address or location RequiredEnter a useful location such as street, area, town or community. Use enough detail for follow-up according to facility practice.
Emergency contact OptionalEnter the contact person's name and phone number in this field when appropriate. Obtain the patient's permission and follow your facility's rules for recording another person's details.
Accuracy and privacy

Check names, date of birth and phone number before continuing. Avoid entering unrelated personal details. Do not leave the workspace visible to unauthorised people.

Moving to the next step

Select Continue to symptoms. The app checks required patient-detail fields before moving on. If a field is missing or invalid, an error appears beneath it; correct the field and continue. You can return to this step using Back or the step navigation.

The email field is optional. The app requires a first name, last name, date of birth, sex, phone number and location to continue.

ENT-Care Hospital Workspace Step 2 of 3
02

Symptoms and concerns

Describe the reason for the visit and select the affected areas and symptoms.

FieldWhat to enter
Main concern RequiredSummarise the main reason for the visit in the patient's own words where possible. Include the main symptom and relevant context such as side or timing if reported. The app requires at least five characters. Avoid diagnostic conclusions.
Affected area RequiredSelect every area related to the current concern: Ear, Nose and/or Throat. Choose all that apply; selecting an area reveals its symptom choices. At least one area is required.
Symptoms reported RequiredSelect each symptom the patient reports from the choices shown for the selected area(s). At least one symptom must be selected. If a symptom is not listed, describe it in “Other symptoms or details.”
Other symptoms or details OptionalRecord additional symptoms or useful detail not captured by the selections. Be factual and concise; include duration, frequency or side when the patient reports it.
When did it begin? OptionalRecord the patient's description of onset or duration, such as “about four days ago,” “gradually over several weeks,” or “comes and goes.” Do not convert an uncertain account into a precise date.
Pain level OptionalSelect the patient's reported pain score from 0 to 10. Zero means no pain and 10 means the worst pain. Leave as “Not reported” if not assessed or the patient cannot provide a score.
Effect on daily activities OptionalNote the effect described by the patient, for example on sleep, work, eating, communication or usual activities. Do not add effects that were not reported or assessed.

Selection tips

  • Select all relevant areas first.
  • Choose only symptoms the patient reports.
  • If you remove an area, its selected symptoms are also removed.
Keep the patient's account distinct from clinical findings.

Use phrases such as “patient reports” for reported symptoms. Record examination findings in the appropriate clinical record, not as if the patient reported them.

ENT-Care Hospital Workspace Step 3 of 3
03

Health history and attachments

Capture relevant background, handle optional images carefully, and check urgent symptoms.

FieldWhat to enter
Medical history OptionalEnter relevant existing conditions, previous ENT concerns, operations or other history that may help the clinician understand this visit. Include only information relevant to care.
Known allergies OptionalList known allergies and the substance or reaction if known. If the patient reports none, record “None known.” Do not leave uncertainty sounding like a confirmed absence.
Current medications OptionalRecord medication names and, if known, dose and frequency. Include prescribed and over-the-counter medicines when relevant. Do not guess missing doses.
Previous treatment OptionalNote treatment tried for the current concern and whether the patient says it helped. Include approximate timing when useful and known.

Images of the affected area

Images are optional. Add them only when clinically relevant, permitted by facility policy and supported by the patient's explicit informed consent. The interface accepts up to three JPG, PNG or WebP images, each under 4 MB.

  • Use an image of the relevant clinical area only; avoid unnecessary identifying content.
  • Confirm the patient understands that the image will be sent for online analysis and may be retained for quality improvement or specialist review as described in the Terms of Use.
  • Image selection does not itself run analysis or send the image. The separate analysis workflow asks for consent again before images are submitted.
  • Images are kept for the current intake session and are not saved in the encrypted local intake record. Remove an image from the session if it was selected in error.
Never upload an image without the required consent.

If consent is not provided, leave the image out. Complete the intake using the available text fields and follow facility procedure.

Immediate attention check

Ask whether any of the listed symptoms are present. Select every symptom the patient has. The form lists:

Severe difficulty breathing
Uncontrolled bleeding
Sudden hearing loss
Severe throat swelling
If a potential emergency symptom is present, arrange immediate clinical assessment.

Do not wait for a routine appointment or delay emergency care to finish the intake or run an analysis. Follow your institution's emergency protocol.

ENT-Care Hospital Workspace Review, save and continue care

Completion and information handling

Review before completing

Take a moment to check the record with the patient or their representative, where appropriate. Correct errors before finishing.

Patient name and date of birth are accurate.
Contact details are complete and correctly spelled.
Main concern reflects what the patient reported.
At least one affected area and symptom are selected.
Relevant history is factual; unknown details are not guessed.
Any image has the required consent and is relevant.
Urgent symptoms have been acted on under facility protocol.
Information collected is limited to what is needed for care.

Save and continue

  1. Draft saving: the form saves intake details securely on this device as you work. Check the status near the top of the page; if it reports a save problem, do not assume the draft was saved.
  2. Complete intake: select this button on Step 3 to save the completed visit locally. The app then displays the clinical-analysis workspace.
  3. Analysis is separate: completing the intake does not automatically send it to the service or run analysis. A clinician must choose to run online clinical analysis. Review the patient's consent and the on-screen image consent before submitting any images.
  4. Review or print: use the saved visits area to reopen local records. The print blank form action creates a paper copy for handwriting; protect completed copies as confidential patient records.
Local records and connectivity

Patient intake and saved reports are encrypted on this device. Online analysis and other online services require internet connectivity. Follow your institution's device security, retention, access and incident-reporting policies.

For account or device support, contact your hospital administrator or the ENT-Care support contact provided by your organisation. For clinical decisions, follow your facility's clinical and emergency protocols.
