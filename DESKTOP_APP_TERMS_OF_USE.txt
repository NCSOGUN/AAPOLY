3. CLINICAL RESPONSIBILITY

3.1  DECISION SUPPORT ONLY. All analysis results, suggested diagnoses,
     urgency scores, red flags, and clinical recommendations produced by
     this platform are decision support tools only. They are NOT confirmed
     clinical diagnoses.

3.2  A qualified and registered medical professional must independently
     assess every patient. The platform's output must not be relied upon
     as the sole basis for any clinical decision, prescription, referral,
     or treatment.

3.3  You remain solely and fully responsible for all clinical decisions
     made in relation to patients you assess, regardless of what the
     platform suggests.

3.4  The AI and database matching system may produce incorrect, incomplete,
     or misleading outputs. You must apply your own professional judgement
     to evaluate every result.

3.5  For emergency presentations — including but not limited to sudden
     hearing loss, airway compromise, severe bleeding, neck swelling
     affecting breathing, or facial nerve palsy — you must follow your
     institution's emergency protocols. Do not delay emergency care in
     order to run a platform analysis.

3.6  All automated findings displayed in the application must be clearly
     communicated to patients as computer-assisted assessments, not as
     confirmed diagnoses, before any treatment decision is made.

4. PATIENT CONSENT AND CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1  You must obtain appropriate informed consent from each patient before
     entering their information into this application. This includes
     consent for:
       (a) their symptoms and clinical history to be processed by the
           platform's AI and database systems;
       (b) any clinical images (ear canal, throat, nasal cavity, etc.)
           to be uploaded and analysed;
       (c) their data to be transmitted to the ENT-Care server for
           analysis purposes.

4.2  You must inform patients that:
       (a) uploaded clinical images may be retained by the platform for
           diagnostic quality improvement purposes;
       (b) a tracking ID is generated for each analysis report and may
           be used to retrieve that report in future;
       (c) data is processed under Nigerian data protection law.

4.3  You must access patient information only when it is necessary for
     the clinical care of that specific patient. Accessing records out
     of curiosity, for research without ethical approval, or for any
     purpose unrelated to direct patient care is prohibited.

4.4  You must follow your organisation's policies on data retention,
     incident reporting, and information governance at all times.

4.5  You must not photograph, copy, or export patient data from the
     application for any purpose not authorised by your institution
     and by applicable law.

5. IMAGE UPLOAD AND CLINICAL PHOTOGRAPHY

5.1  Clinical images submitted through this application (otoscope images,
     throat photographs, nasal images) will be transmitted to the ENT-Care
     server and stored securely in encrypted cloud storage for analysis
     and diagnostic quality review.

5.2  You must obtain explicit patient consent before capturing and
     uploading any clinical photograph. This consent must be documented
     in accordance with your institution's procedures.

5.3  Images must be of the clinical area relevant to the patient's
     presenting complaint only. You must not submit images that identify
     the patient in any way beyond clinical necessity.

5.4  The platform provides AI-assisted analysis of clinical images.
     This analysis is preliminary and must be validated by clinical
     examination. The disclaimer provided with every image analysis
     result must be communicated to the patient.

5.5  Patients may request deletion of their submitted images by
     contacting support@aapoly.edu.ng.

6. LOCAL DATA STORAGE AND CONNECTIVITY

6.1  This application stores certain data locally on the device on which
     it is installed. This includes your authentication token, your
     facility information, and any patient intake details or reports
     created during offline sessions.

6.2  Locally stored data is encrypted. You must ensure the device
     running this application is protected by a device-level password or
     PIN at all times.

6.3  Diagnosis and analysis features require an active internet connection.
     The application will indicate clearly when these features are
     unavailable due to lack of connectivity.

6.4  When connectivity is restored, the application may synchronise
     queued data with the ENT-Care server. You are responsible for
     ensuring that any data queued during offline use is accurate and
     complete before synchronisation occurs.

6.5  You must not install this application on personal devices that do
     not meet your institution's security and device management policies.

7. ESCALATED CASES AND SPECIALIST REVIEW

7.1  The platform automatically identifies certain analyses as complex
     cases and flags them for specialist review. These cases are stored
     on the ENT-Care server and may be reviewed by qualified specialist
     personnel connected to the platform.

7.2  Patient consent must be obtained before a case is escalated for
     specialist review. The consent mechanism provided within the
     application must be used for this purpose.

7.3  Responses provided by specialists through the escalation system are
     advisory in nature. The treating clinician retains full responsibility
     for the final clinical decision.

7.4  Escalated cases are retained on the server in accordance with the
     data retention policy set out in Section 10 of these terms.

10.8  In the event of a data breach affecting patient data, ENT-Care
      will notify affected parties and the Nigeria Data Protection
      Commission (NDPC) in accordance with applicable law.
